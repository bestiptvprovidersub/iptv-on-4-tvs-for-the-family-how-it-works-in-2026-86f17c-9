IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) lets you watch TV channels over your home internet connection. In 2026, a family can use one subscription to stream on 4 different TVs at the same time.

You just need a fast internet plan, compatible devices, and an IPTV app.

What is IPTV and how does it work?

Instead of cable or satellite, IPTV sends video as data through your broadband. A premium service like SubTV IP (subtvip.store) uses powerful servers to deliver 4K/60 fps HEVC streams. HEVC is a video format that gives sharp images without needing huge internet speed.

Each TV in your home gets its own stream. You can watch different channels on each screen, all at once. The service handles up to 4 simultaneous connections without slowdowns.

Setting up IPTV on 4 TVs in 2026

You do not need a single box for all TVs. Instead, each TV uses its own device or built‑in smart TV system. The steps are the same for every screen.

You can mix and match. One TV might use a Fire Stick, another an Android box.

These apps are free or low cost. They let you enter the subscription details you receive after purchase.

Step 3: Activate your subscription

Buy a plan (1, 3, or 12 months) from SubTV IP. The activation is instant. You get a username, password, and server URL. Enter them in the app on each TV. That’s it – all four TVs start working immediately.

How to avoid buffering when using 4 TVs

Streaming on four screens at once uses a lot of bandwidth. Here is how to keep playback smooth in 2026:

  • Use an internet plan with at least 50 Mbps download speed.
  • Connect each TV via Ethernet cable if possible. Wi‑Fi can work but wired is more stable.
  • Set your DNS to Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1). This helps prevent your internet provider from slowing down streaming traffic – a trick explained by BleepingComputer in their guide on ISP throttling.
  • Adjust the buffer or cache setting in your player app to 5,000 ms. This gives the stream time to load ahead.

The premium service itself has a 99.9% uptime guarantee and an anti‑freeze server cluster, so problems are rare when your network is set up right.

Frequently asked questions about IPTV on 4 TVs

How many TVs can I use with one IPTV subscription?

A premium plan like SubTV IP allows you to watch on 4 different TVs at the same time. Each TV uses its own stream, so you can watch different content on each screen.

What internet speed do I need for 4 TVs?

At least 50 Mbps download speed is recommended. This gives each TV about 12 Mbps, which is enough for 4K streams. Slower speeds may cause buffering on some screens.

Do I need a separate box for each TV?

Yes, each TV needs its own device – like a Fire TV Stick, Android TV box, or Apple TV – unless the TV already has a compatible smart platform (Samsung Tizen or LG webOS).

Can I watch on different devices at the same time?

Yes. You can use a Fire Stick in the living room, an Android box in the bedroom, and a smart TV in the kitchen – all at once. The subscription does not lock you to one device type.

How do I set up IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV?

If your TV runs Tizen OS, install an IPTV player app like IPTV Smarters Pro from the Samsung App Store. Then enter the subscription details you received. The same steps apply to LG webOS TVs.

What is an anti‑freeze server cluster?

It is a group of servers that work together to keep video playing smoothly. If one server gets busy, another takes over instantly. This prevents the “freezing” or spinning wheel that sometimes happens with lower‑quality services.

Is 4K streaming available on all 4 TVs?

Yes, as long as each TV and device supports 4K resolution. The premium subscription delivers 4K / 60 fps HEVC streams to every screen that can handle it.
